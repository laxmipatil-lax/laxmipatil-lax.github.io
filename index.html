About me

I’m a final-year MCA student from Belagavi, Karnataka. I like software that works for real people, which is why my internship on a medical billing product mattered to me: healthcare providers rely on it every day.

My strongest habit is breaking a problem apart. I read the code, follow the issue through the database and the interface, find where it starts, and write down what I found so the next person doesn’t have to guess.

Lately I’ve been building with generative AI: a document chatbot that runs fully on my own machine, and a research agent that checks its own answers. I enjoy making AI answers traceable to a source.

I’m looking for a first role in software development, AI/ML, or application support, where I can keep learning from a strong team.
